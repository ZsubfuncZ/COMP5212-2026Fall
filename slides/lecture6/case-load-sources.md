# Electricity-demand case

Primary source: Ben Taieb and Hyndman (2014), A gradient boosting approach to the Kaggle load forecasting competition. Author-hosted paper: https://robjhyndman.com/papers/kaggle-competition.pdf . Abstract, Section 3, Section 4.2, and conclusion. Checked 2026-09-24.

The two native figures are explanatory diagrams. No demand observations, error curves or comparative scores are invented. The only reported performance quantity is the competition rank. The information-availability takeaway is a teaching inference, not a description of the paper's cross-validation implementation.
